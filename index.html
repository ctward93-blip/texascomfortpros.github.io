AC Repair FAQ

How much does AC repair cost in Houston?▼

Most AC repairs in Houston range from $150–$550. Minor issues like capacitor replacement run $150–$250. Refrigerant recharge runs $200–$400. We give you the flat price after diagnosis before doing any work.

Is it worth repairing an old AC unit?▼

HVAC Installation Done Right the First Time

A new HVAC system is one of the biggest investments a Houston homeowner makes. At Texas Comfort Pros, we engineer your installation — right sizing, proper ductwork evaluation, and quality brands that last — not just the cheapest box we can bolt in and walk away from.

Signs You Need a New System

  • Your unit is 12–15+ years old

  • Drastically rising energy bills despite maintenance
  • Inconsistent temperatures room to room
  • R-22 (Freon) system — the refrigerant is now illegal to produce

Duct Work — Cypress, Katy & Cinco Ranch

Leaky or poorly insulated ductwork is one of the most common and overlooked causes of high energy bills and uneven cooling in Houston-area homes. Many Cypress and Katy homes built in the 80s and 90s have flex duct that has deteriorated, disconnected, or was never properly sealed to begin with.

Texas Comfort Pros handles:

  • Duct inspection and leakage testing
  • Duct sealing with mastic compound or foil tape
  • Damaged section repair or full segment replacement
  • Complete duct system replacement for older homes
  • Attic duct insulation upgrades
  • Zoning improvements for uneven cooling
  • Air handler plenum and boot repairs

Fix your ducts and your AC will cool more effectively for the same — or lower — electricity cost. It's often the cheapest upgrade with the biggest payoff.

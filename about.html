About Texas Comfort Pros — Meet Corey

Licensed, local, and honest. The HVAC technician Houston homeowners actually want to call.

Not a Call Center. Just Corey.

Texas Comfort Pros was built on a straightforward idea: Houston homeowners deserve a licensed HVAC technician they can actually trust — not a commissioned sales tech from a national franchise, and not someone dispatched by a call center three cities away.

When you call Texas Comfort Pros, Corey picks up. He comes to your home, diagnoses the problem himself, gives you a straight answer, and fixes it. No upselling. No disappearing after the sale. No rotating cast of techs who've never seen your system before.

Corey is TDLR licensed and EPA 608 certified, with hands-on experience across the full range of residential AC systems common in Cypress, Katy, Copperfield, and Northwest Houston homes — single-stage, two-stage, high-efficiency, mini-split, and multi-zone setups.

The business stays lean by design. Lower overhead means fair prices for customers and work Corey stands behind personally — because his name is on every single job.
